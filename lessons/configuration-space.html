This lab takes about two hours in pairs. By the end you can name the six basic joints, count the degrees of freedom (DoF) of a mechanism with a formula, and check your count on a mechanism you built yourself.

Your kit holds printed joints (revolute, prismatic, helical, cylindrical, universal, spherical), links with holes every 10 mm, and a base plate. Work through the activities in order. Each one asks you to predict first, then build, then compare.

  • Write your answers in the boxes, or in your own notes. The boxes are not saved when you close the page.
  • Printed joints have a little play. Ignore the play when you count DoF.
  • When your prediction and your observation disagree, do not erase either one. Write down why.
